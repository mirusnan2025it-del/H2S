Important Safety Notice

This prototype analyzes the visible color response of an H₂S indicator strip. It does not directly measure or verify gas concentration in ppm.

Use only prepared/reference indicator strips or the software simulator for demonstrations. Do not generate or handle H₂S gas for testing.

🛡️
Responsible Use

The wristband prototype is a demonstration and decision-support concept. Follow your workplace safety procedures and do not rely on this prototype as a substitute for certified gas-monitoring equipment.
